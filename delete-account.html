SkyMet account and data deletion

SkyMet and Hexagon Technology let users permanently delete their account and the associated cloud data.

Deleting in the app

Go to Menu > Account and Sync > Delete Cloud Account Permanently. After you confirm, your Firebase account and SkyMet cloud backup are deleted.

If you can’t access the app

Use the link below to send a request to the support address from the Google email address you used for your account, without changing the subject.

Send an account deletion request

Additional information may be requested to verify account ownership. For verified requests, the Firebase user account and the associated SkyMet cloud document are permanently deleted.

Data deleted and kept

The Google-linked Firebase Authentication account and the Cloud Firestore backup of favorites, the selected location and app preferences are deleted. If the app is installed on a device, local settings may remain on the device until the app data is cleared or the app is uninstalled.
