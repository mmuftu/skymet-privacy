SkyMet Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 12, 2026
Türkçe

Data controller

SkyMet is developed by Hexagon Technology. For privacy and data requests, contact mustafamuftuoglu@gmail.com.

Data processed

To show local weather, the app processes the device’s precise or approximate location; when the user allows it, the Google account name and email address; favorite places, app settings, notification preferences, map and precipitation analysis preferences; and favorite cameras stored on the device.

How data is used

Location data is used to determine the nearby weather forecast and place name; account information to provide optional cloud sync; and preferences to run the app, the widget and weather notifications according to the user’s choices.

Widget and background location

When device location is selected on the widget and the user also turns on the “Allow all the time” permission, SkyMet gets the current location during widget refreshes about every 15 minutes, even when the app is closed. Coordinates are sent to weather and place-name services. No continuous location tracking or location history is created; the last selected coordinates are kept on the device. This background location request stops when switching back to a favorite place. The permission is optional; if it isn’t granted, the weather for the last selected location is refreshed. The permission can be revoked in Android settings.

Service providers

Weather forecasts, UV, model air quality, dust and pollen data come from Open-Meteo; temperature, pressure, humidity, snow and wind analyses and nearby station observations from NOAA; community air quality measurements from Sensor.Community; place naming from BigDataCloud and OpenStreetMap Nominatim; satellite, lightning and sea wind observations from EUMETSAT; and the map from OpenStreetMap. Precipitation radar is loaded from RainViewer. Technical model maps may come from ECMWF, Meteociel, NOAA/NCEP, DWD or Wetterzentrale, depending on the selected product and availability in the release. Dam data comes from dolulukorani.com, DSİ and, for Ankara, ASKİ. Online weather photos come from Pexels, and sea and wave forecasts from Open-Meteo Marine. In the Elevation and Terrain layer, terrain tiles come from Mapzen Terrain Tiles on Amazon Web Services, and the approximate elevation of a tapped point from Open-Meteo Elevation. Google Firebase Authentication and Cloud Firestore are used for authentication and cloud backup when the user explicitly connects a Google account. Providers that receive network requests may receive the IP address and request information; for location-based weather and place-name queries, coordinates are also sent.

Live cameras

Streams in the Live Cameras layer belong to municipalities, institutions and other broadcasters. When you choose to watch a stream in the app, the video loads directly from the broadcaster’s server or YouTube’s official embedded player; external links open in your browser. During this, the broadcaster or YouTube (Google) may receive your IP address and device and request information; the YouTube player may use its own cookies and similar technologies and is subject to Google’s privacy policy. SkyMet doesn’t record camera images or link the streams you watch to an account. Favorite cameras are stored only on your device. Streams open only when you choose them.

Free to use

SkyMet is free; it contains no in-app purchases, subscriptions or ads.

Retention and security

Local preferences are kept on the device until the app is uninstalled or its data is cleared. Cloud data is kept on Firebase until the account is deleted. Network communication is encrypted with HTTPS. SkyMet doesn’t sell personal data, and this version of the app contains no ads.

User choices

Location and notification permissions can be revoked in Android or Windows settings. Connecting a Google account is optional; basic weather features can be used without an account.

Account and data deletion

In the app, Menu > Account and Sync > Delete Cloud Account Permanently permanently deletes the Firebase user account and the associated SkyMet cloud backup. Local settings on the device are kept until the user clears the app data or uninstalls the app.

If you can’t access the app, use the external account deletion request page.

Children’s privacy

SkyMet is not specifically directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly aim to collect personal data from children.

Policy changes

This policy is updated when the app’s data use changes. The current version is published on this page. If the Turkish and English versions differ, the Turkish version prevails.
